Privacy Policy — Poker RPG

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Poker RPG ("the Game") is a single-player card-based role-playing game offered by SagaForge Interactive ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game or contact us.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores information needed for gameplay and your preferences on your device, including saved progress, character progression, deck and card collection, in-game resources, and sound/language preferences. It may also store queued analytics events and a randomly generated installation identifier locally. Removing the Game or clearing its app data generally removes this locally stored information, subject to your device's backup settings.

2. Gameplay and technical analytics

When analytics transmission is enabled for the Game build you use, the Game may send the following information to our own analytics service:

  • A randomly generated application-installation identifier and session identifiers; dates and durations of sessions.
  • Game version, build, platform, and technical/error events.
  • Gameplay events, such as tutorial progress, map movement, battles, cards used, rewards, upgrades, level and deck-size changes, and use of in-game shops.
  • Campaign, referral, or creative identifiers if acquisition attribution is available.

The installation identifier is pseudonymous, not your name. However, it can link game events from the same installation across sessions. We do not request your real name, email address, phone number, or contact list to play the Game. An internet connection can disclose an IP address and related technical request metadata to network or hosting providers when requests are delivered; IP addresses are not deliberately included in the game's gameplay-event payloads.

3. How we use information

We use game data to save progress, operate the Game, understand onboarding and retention, balance gameplay, investigate errors, measure broad campaign performance where enabled, and improve stability and user experience. We do not sell player analytics data.

4. Purchases and advertising

The current test scope does not include active third-party advertising. If in-app purchases are enabled in a future version, payment processing is handled by Google Play; we may receive purchase status, product and transaction references necessary to deliver and restore purchased in-game content. We do not receive payment-card numbers. If ads or new external services are added, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before those features are released.

5. Sharing and service providers

Gameplay analytics may be processed on infrastructure operated for us by service providers (such as hosting or network service providers), solely to run and secure the relevant services. Google Play separately processes information associated with installation, distribution, and any purchases under Google's own policies. We do not share gameplay information with independent advertisers for personalized ad profiling in the current test scope. Specific providers may change as we prepare the production hosting environment, and this policy will be updated if our practices materially change.

6. Data security

For externally accessible analytics services, we intend to use encrypted HTTPS connections and access controls. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Release builds must be checked to ensure test-only analytics endpoints and credentials are not used for public testing.

7. Retention and deletion

Local save data and preferences generally remain on your device until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall it. Server-side analytics records are kept while needed for usage analysis, troubleshooting, and legitimate operational purposes; there is not currently a fixed automatic deletion period. You can request access to or deletion of analytics associated with your installation by emailing us. We will assess the request, take reasonable steps to locate and remove applicable data, and explain if we cannot match a request to a specific installation or if legal requirements require retention.

8. Your choices and rights

You may clear local game data using your device settings. To ask questions or request access or deletion of analytics records, email sagaforge.interactive@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, data protection laws may give you additional rights. We will respond to requests as required by applicable law.

9. Changes

We may revise this Privacy Policy when we change gameplay services, analytics, purchases, advertising, or other data practices. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.
