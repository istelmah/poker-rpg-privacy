Request Data Deletion for Poker RPG

Last updated: October 23, 2026

If you play Poker RPG, you can ask SagaForge Interactive to delete the game analytics data that we can identify as associated with your installation. This page is accessible without signing in, and you do not need to reinstall the game to contact us.

No game account is required. Poker RPG does not currently offer creation of, or sign-in to, a game account. Requests on this page concern game data, not deletion of a user account.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to sagaforge.interactive@gmail.com with the subject Poker RPG — Data Deletion Request.
  2. Tell us that you would like your Poker RPG analytics data deleted. If you know your game installation or analytics identifier, please include it; this helps us find the correct records. If you do not know it, you can still send your request. You may include the approximate period when you played and the game version, if known.
  3. We will respond to your request and explain whether we can identify the relevant records, whether we need a non-sensitive detail to prevent deleting another person's data, and what action has been taken. We will handle requests within applicable legal requirements.
Email a data deletion request

Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, identity documents, or other sensitive information. An email address is only needed for us to receive and reply to your request.

What data can be deleted?

  • Server-side game analytics, where identifiable: pseudonymous installation/user identifiers, session records, gameplay and progression events, technical event details, and related analytics user properties associated with that identifier.
  • Data on your device: save games, in-game progress, deck and character preferences, and any locally queued analytics are stored locally. You can remove them using Android Settings → Apps → Poker RPG → Storage → Clear data (names vary by device), or by uninstalling the game, subject to your device backup settings. We cannot remotely remove local files from your device.
  • Other records: we may need to keep limited correspondence about your request or information retained for legal or security reasons; we will explain any applicable exceptions. Information that cannot reasonably be linked back to an installation, such as aggregate statistics, may not be removable as individual records.

Retention

Poker RPG does not currently have a fixed automatic deletion period for server-side analytics. Records are kept while needed for gameplay analysis, troubleshooting and legitimate operations unless deleted after a valid request or otherwise removed. Local gameplay data generally remains until you clear the application's data or uninstall it. We will tell you about any legal retention requirements relevant to your request.

What if I do not know my installation identifier?

You may still request deletion by email using the button above. Because Poker RPG does not require an account or collect your email during gameplay, we might be unable to reliably match an email to past pseudonymous analytics records without an installation identifier. In that case, we will explain the limitation and any available options; we will not ask you to provide unnecessary sensitive personal information.
